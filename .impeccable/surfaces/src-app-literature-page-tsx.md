Scope: the panel-facing literature review that establishes the evidence behind Cotton Grade without turning the page into a report chapter. Visitor mode: Read.

Audience and job: an academic review panel must understand the three foundations—plant vision, transferable features and cotton imaging—and see exactly why the final controlled experiment is still needed.

Proof and constraints: show six real primary studies with direct source links. Summarize what each study did, its useful finding and its limitation or connection. Describe the gap as limited evidence for the exact progressive cotton-domain sequence; never claim that no related work exists.

Direction: use the Architecture page's restrained research visual world, projector-scale headings and generous paper-white spacing. The research-gap sequence is the memorable visual; citations remain concise and scannable.

Interaction: sources open in a new tab. All core reasoning stays visible, with no hover-only evidence or ornamental animation.
